Hands-On Craft & Creative Disciplines

Before I ever wrote code, I worked with my hands. Building physical things, growing food, singing, and drawing taught me patience, focus, and how things fit together. When I sit down to program, that same practical mindset carries straight through.

Woodworking & Framing

Woodworking & Framing in Progress

Putting together a custom frame with hand tools, measuring tape, and stretch fabric. Making sure every corner is square and solid before anything else goes on. Working with raw wood teaches you that structure is everything: if your base is not level, nothing you build on top of it will hold up.

Finished Build

Finished Custom Seat Build

The completed royal blue upholstered seat cushion, built from raw lumber all the way up. Taking a project from rough boards and foam to a clean, comfortable, finished seat is one of the best feelings there is. It is that same satisfaction I get when an application comes together and runs cleanly from end to end.

Handcrafted Wood & Art

Handcrafted Painted Wood Sign

Handcrafted wooden slat sign built from individual pine planks, painted with warm color transitions and John 15:1. Combining carpentry with painting, lettering, and botanical leaves. Working on raw wood means sanding down rough grain, priming the surface, and hand-lettering each line so the colors stay vibrant outdoors. It brings together woodworking, painting, and a grounded reminder about growth and pruning.

Carpentry & Garden Build

Raised Bed Planter & Seedling Starts

Sitting back after building a raised wooden planter box and getting early seedling starts settled in their pots. Building your own garden beds from lumber means measuring corners, securing brackets, and preparing good soil. Starting seeds from scratch takes daily consistency: checking moisture, watching for the first green shoots, and giving them time to establish strong root systems before planting them out in the yard.

Gardening & Soil

Gardening & Backyard Tomatoes

Checking on the tomato plants out in the garden. Getting outside in the dirt and watching things grow keeps me grounded. Plants do not care about shortcuts: you test the soil, watch the leaves, water them consistently, and let time do its thing. It is a daily reminder that good work takes steady attention.

Gardening & Flowers

Backyard Marigolds & Garden Corner

A quiet morning in the garden with the watering can, potted herbs, and vibrant yellow and orange marigolds in full bloom next to a rainbow-painted wood board. Marigolds are great companion plants that protect the garden naturally. Taking time every morning to water and check on the plants is my favorite way to clear my head before diving into code and systems architecture.

Studio Workbench

Art Studio & Code Workbench

My everyday workspace where painting and coding happen side by side. On the left is my laptop with the terminal and code editor; on the right are my paints, jars, palette, and brushes. I like having both worlds in one room. Working with visual mediums keeps my mind flexible when I am solving software problems.

Painting & Color Work

Studio Painting & Color Palette

Mixing paints right on the palette in the studio. Working with physical pigment teaches you about values and layers: you lay down your base tones first, build up midtones, and save high-contrast highlights for last. Whether I am blending colors on canvas or styling clean web layouts, it all comes down to how shapes and tones balance each other out.

Hand-Drawn Art

Drawing & Sacred Geometry

Hand-drawn geometric patterns drawn with a ruler, compass, and pencil. Drafting balanced shapes by hand trains your eye for spacing, radius, and proportion. When I build front-end layouts or SVG charts, that same spatial awareness helps me make screens that feel natural and easy to navigate.

Music & Singing

Singing & Vocal Harmonics

Singing into the mic and working with sound. Singing trains you to listen closely, hit steady pitches, and pay attention to how frequencies fill a space. That fascination with sound is what pushed me to build Web Audio tools, browser synthesizers, and real-time frequency visualizers.

Atmospheric Photography

St. Louis Twilight & Atmospheric Light

Catching the sunset over the St. Louis metropolitan area with amber, rose, and twilight blue cloud layers. Paying attention to how natural light shifts through the atmosphere trains your eye for color temperature, ambient gradients, and contrast. Those same color relationships feed right back into how I build responsive UI themes, SVG charts, and interactive canvas visualizers.
